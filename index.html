Itay Hazan

PhD Student · Tel Aviv University

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Tel Aviv University, advised by Prof. Lior Wolf. I work on generative models, with a focus on diffusion and flow matching.

My research spans images, video, text, and robotics. Much of it asks what a pretrained generator already knows, and how to use that at inference time, without retraining, to make sampling faster, motion more coherent, and outputs more faithful to the prompt.

Before my PhD, I completed my BSc and MSc at the Technion. My MSc, under the supervision of Prof. Eyal Kushilevitz, was in complexity theory.

Fellow, TAD Excellence Program for Doctoral Students in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, 2026–2027.
